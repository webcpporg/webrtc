[#webrtc-guarantees]
== Guarantees

What webrtc promises, and the tests that hold each promise, which fail when
the promise breaks. A guarantee keeps its number, and the anchor named after
it, so a comment of the code or of a test cites it by that anchor:
`+(doc: #webrtc-invariant-3)+` sends its reader to the third. The tests are
those of <<webrtc-testing>>.

. [[webrtc-invariant-1]]*Two peers connect and talk.* Two native peers on
the loopback exchange their descriptions and every candidate, none refused,
reach `connected`, and carry binary and text messages both ways, each read
with whether it was text; so do two peers in one page of a browser. _Held
by_ `native` (`a_full_exchange`), the `browser` lane (`browser_test`,
`a_full_exchange`) and every example of the page.
. [[webrtc-invariant-2]]*Every handler runs on the object's executor, and
never inside the call that started its operation.* An operation satisfied at
once is posted; nothing of libdatachannel's threads runs a handler. _Held by_
`events` (`a_satisfied_wait_is_posted`), `native`, and `lifetime` under
ThreadSanitizer; in the browser by `browser_imports`
(`resolutions_deliver_their_outcome`), whose imports never re-enter {cpp}.
. [[webrtc-invariant-3]]*No event is lost without a rule.* While no wait is
pending, states coalesce to the latest, candidates queue up to their bound
with a place kept for the end of gathering, and incoming channels queue up to
theirs; a second wait of a kind fails with `already_started`, and a cancelled
wait consumes nothing. _Held by_ `events` (`states_coalesce`,
`no_change_keeps_waiting`, `one_wait_of_a_kind`,
`a_cancelled_wait_consumes_nothing`, `the_bound_keeps_a_place_for_the_end`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-4]]*A channel keeps what it received before its first
read.* Messages are kept from the channel's creation until `async_receive`
reads them, in order. _Held by_ `native` (`received_before_the_first_receive`)
and the `browser` lane (`received_before_the_first_receive`,
`accepted_after_its_messages`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-5]]*`close()` delivers no state change.* A closed
connection's pending operations complete with `operation_aborted`, its
channels close with `error::closed`, and a later wait fails with
`error::closed`; a channel closed by its owner completes its
`async_wait_close`. _Held by_ `native` (`close_fires_no_state_change`,
`a_channel_closed_by_its_owner`) and `events`
(`close_aborts_and_then_refuses`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-6]]*`run()` returns once the connections are closed.* A
pending operation is outstanding work, and a program that starts its waits
again keeps `run()` running until it closes its connection. _Held by_
`native` (`run_returns_once_closed`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-7]]*Every failure is a value.* Of the category
`webcpp.webrtc`, whose ten values are fixed, or one of Asio's; nothing
throws, with exceptions on or off. _Held by_ `error`, `native`
(`errors_as_values`) and the example `webrtc_errors`.
. [[webrtc-invariant-8]]*A refused send or receive leaves the channel as it
was.* A send past the message size or the buffered amount fails and the
channel stays open; a message larger than the room left in the caller's
buffer fails with `no_buffer_space` and stays queued, then reads into a
larger buffer. _Held by_ `native` (`bounds`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-9]]*The native implementation works without
exceptions.* Built with `exception-handling=off`, against libdatachannel's
archives built with exceptions, it behaves as with them. _Held by_
`native_no_exceptions`, `events_no_exceptions`, and every header compiled
alone without exceptions. The browser implementation works without them
too: every emscripten test and every program of the `browser` lane runs
again with `exception-handling=off`.
. [[webrtc-invariant-10]]*The object's memory is its allocator's, on its
executor.* Its state, queues and outputs are allocated through it, an
operation's state through its handler's allocator, and nothing of the
object's allocator is touched on libdatachannel's threads. _Held by_
`allocation` (`state_queues_and_outputs_in_the_allocator`) and `events`
(`an_operation_is_in_its_handlers_allocator`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-11]]*Destruction is safe while events arrive.* A
connection and its channels destroyed while the peer floods them touch
nothing afterwards, round after round. _Held by_ `lifetime`
(`destroyed_while_messages_arrive`), 100 rounds under ThreadSanitizer.
. [[webrtc-invariant-12]]*A channel never read stalls its connection's
others, and its destruction releases them.* Past 1,024 unread messages,
natively, a message on another channel of the connection does not arrive;
destroying the flooded channel releases it, and returns in under two
seconds. _Held by_ `stall` and `lifetime`
(`a_flooded_channel_is_destroyed_promptly`), so that a change of
libdatachannel's behaviour fails them and this page changes with it.
. [[webrtc-invariant-13]]*The runtime caps the connections alive at once.*
`make` past the cap fails with `too_many_peer_connections`, and a
connection's place follows its moves and is given back when it is
destroyed. _Held by_ `native` (`errors_as_values`, `destruction_and_moves`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-14]]*A negotiated channel opens on both peers with the
same id, and neither accepts it.* _Held by_ `native` (`a_negotiated_channel`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-15]]*In the browser, unread messages never pass their
budget.* A channel's unread messages and their records stay within
`options::max_received_bytes`, charged as the browser delivers them; past
it, or past `max_message_size`, the channel closes and `async_receive`
completes with `receive_overflow` or `message_too_large`. _Held by_
`browser_backend` (`messages_and_the_buffer`) and the `browser` lane
(`unread_messages_past_the_budget`, `a_received_message_past_the_size`).
. [[webrtc-invariant-16]]*Translation units that disagree on the unsupported
policy do not link together.* The browser's classes live in an inline
namespace named after the policy, so a channel passed between two such units
names two different types. _Held by_ `config_boundary`, which links, and
`config_mismatch`, which must not.
