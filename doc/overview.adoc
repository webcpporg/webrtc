[#webrtc-overview]
== Overview

WebRTC connects two programs directly, through the networks between them,
once each has told the other how to reach it. webrtc gives a {cpp} program
the data-channel half of it: a `peer_connection` negotiates the connection
with the other peer, and a `data_channel` carries messages over it, text or
binary, reliable and ordered or not.

The names are the W3C's, so that a reader who knows
`RTCPeerConnection.setLocalDescription()` knows
`peer_connection::async_set_local_description`, and the semantics are the
W3C's where a platform allows them, with every difference stated in
<<webrtc-differences>>. The model is Asio's:

* every operation is an `async_*` member that takes any completion token, a
  callback, `use_awaitable`, `use_future` or a deferred token, and runs its
  handler on the object's executor, `webcpp::asio::any_io_executor` unless
  the program chooses another;
* what the W3C delivers as an event, a candidate, a state change, an incoming
  channel, a message, is a wait the program starts and starts again, as an
  acceptor's `async_accept` (<<webrtc-events>>);
* every failure is a value, an `error_code` of the category `webcpp.webrtc`
  or one of Asio's (<<webrtc-errors>>), and every bound webcpp sets is a
  named option that fails as a value, never by throwing or blocking.

It runs natively today, over libdatachannel 0.24.6, which the superproject
builds from its pinned sources (<<webrtc-native>>), with exceptions on and
off. The browser implementation, over the page's own `RTCPeerConnection`
through webcpp::asio's bridge, comes in the library's next increment, with
a lane where a native peer and a browser peer talk to each other; until then
a header that needs a backend stops with `#error` on emscripten. WASI has no
WebRTC, neither in a guest nor from a host, so there the headers stop with an
`#error` that says so.

Media, the tracks, codecs and transceivers of WebRTC, is not part of
webrtc: it will get an interface of its own. Nor is any integration with
another library: an actor of webcpp::xactor that needs a peer holds a
`peer_connection` made on the executor its driver runs on and turns each
completion into a message to itself.
